A Helpful Place for Students

StudentHub brings together academic and campus information into one accessible portal designed for students and faculty members.

Students can check their subject attendance, weekly lecture timetable, uploaded assignments, exam results, and college circulars.

Our platform is completely responsive and optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Accessibility First

Built with semantic HTML5 elements and keyboard-navigable structures for all users.

Interactive UI

Featuring collapsible FAQs, dynamic event sliders, responsive menus, and light/dark mode persistence.

Centralized Information

Single sign-on access to notices, timetable, attendance percentages, and department notifications.
