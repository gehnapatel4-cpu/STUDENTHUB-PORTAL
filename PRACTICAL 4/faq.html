Frequently Asked Questions

Click on any question below to expand or collapse the answer.

Students must maintain at least 80% overall attendance in each course to be eligible to appear for the university end-semester examinations.

You can view your subject-wise marks and grades by opening the Student Dashboard and selecting the Results option.

The weekly timetable is accessible directly from your Dashboard under the Timetable section, displaying lecture slots and lab timings from Monday to Saturday.

Visit the Assignments page from the Dashboard to check active assignment deadlines, guidelines, and submission links provided by the subject faculty.

Click the Dark Mode / Light Mode toggle button in the top navigation bar. Your selection is automatically saved in your browser localStorage and will remain active across page visits.

If you cannot log in or forgot your credentials, visit the Contact Us page or email the campus helpdesk at studenthub@gmail.com.
